Employee SMS opt-in

This page is how employees agree to receive CHC Time Clock texts. This program is for employees only. It is not for customers, vendors, or the public, and it is not used for marketing or promotions.

What these messages are

Messages are time clock prompts and clock-in/clock-out confirmations from the CHC Time Clock number, (405) 709-8415. Employees text IN and a job name to clock in, and OUT to clock out, and receive a short confirmation that the punch was recorded.

Frequency and rates

Message frequency varies. Employees typically receive up to about 4 messages per workday. Msg & data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. You can also text START to the same number to opt in or rejoin.

Consent to receive these texts is not a condition of employment.
