Privacy Policy

CHC Time Clock
Effective Nov 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the CHC Time Clock SMS program collects and uses information. CHC Time Clock is operated by Calvin Hiel, doing business as CHC Construction and Home Repair in Piedmont, OK.

The service

CHC Time Clock is an internal employee SMS time clock at (405) 709-8415. It is used only by employees to clock in and out of work.

Information we collect

When you text the time clock number, we collect your phone number, the content of your messages (such as IN/OUT and a job name), and timestamps. We collect this information solely to log employee hours.

How we use and keep this information

Time clock data is kept in the owner's private records for payroll and job records.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Calvin Hiel at 405-985-7970.
